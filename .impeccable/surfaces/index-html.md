# Surface Brief: Melek Bisnis Curriculum (`index.html` + `fase-1..5.html` + `kamus-pdf.html`)

- **Mode:** `read` (long-form structured educational curriculum, financial glossary, and interactive workbenches)
- **Audience:** Indonesian college students starting from absolute zero in business and finance ("buta tentang bisnis").
- **Source Material:** User-supplied Indonesian PDF of Morgan Housel's *The Psychology of Money* (Pendahuluan, Bab 1-20, Postscript), reconciled for literal machine-translation quirks alongside core small-business and accounting mechanics.

## Direction contract

- **THESIS:** Treats business literacy like an engineered reference manual and financial workbench paired with behavioral stories, refusing generic colorful SaaS card grids, glowing gradients, and fake-casual chatbot copy.
- **OWN-WORLD:** Apple Pro Max Titanium Editorial palette: Titanium Paper (`#F5F5F7` ground, `#FFFFFF` sheet, `#1D1D1F` ink) and Space Black Titanium (`#0A0A0C` ground, `#141416` panel, `#F5F5F7` ink), Desert Titanium (`#755936` / `#D4B48C`) structural hairlines and PDF page markers, and Precision Blue (`#0066CC` / `#4DA3FF`) strictly for interactive controls and links. Native system sans (`SF Pro` / system-ui) paired with serif blockquotes for book passages and tabular monospace strictly for financial equations and readouts.
- **STORY:** The reader moves sequentially from Phase 1 (mental models, problems, and behavioral money traps) through Phase 2 (revenue, COGS/HPP, margin, BEP, cash flow vs profit), Phase 3 (validation, MVP, unit economics, compounding, and tails), Phase 4 (3 financial statements and margin of safety), to Phase 5 (moat, price vs value, and transition to *The Intelligent Investor*), testing every concept directly in interactive calculators and scenario checks.
- **FIRST VIEWPORT:** Compact frosted top bar (brand mark, single-row multi-page nav, theme override toggle), hairline scroll-progress indicator, crisp main `h1` headline with concise framing summary (no decorative eyebrow pill above `h1`), direct phase action links, and an immediate architectural SVG schematic mapping zero-to-investor progression above the sequential 5-phase syllabus index.
- **FORM:** Pinned user direction (Apple 18 Pro Max minimalist editorial reader, `ENERGY 1 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1`), overriding the unassigned catalog roll (`seed:edc9d15409542558`).
- **FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
